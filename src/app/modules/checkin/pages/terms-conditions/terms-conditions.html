Hotel Check-in Terms and Conditions

1. Check-in Requirements

All guests must present a valid government-issued photo ID and the credit card used for booking at check-in. The name on the ID must match the name on the reservation.

2. Age Requirements

Guests must be at least 18 years old to check in. Guests under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian.

3. Payment and Authorization

We will authorize your credit card for the full amount of your stay plus incidentals. The authorization will be released within 3-5 business days after checkout, depending on your bank.

4. Early Check-in

Early check-in is subject to availability and may incur additional fees. Standard check-in time is 3:00 PM.

5. Late Check-in

If you plan to arrive after 11:00 PM, please contact the hotel directly to arrange late check-in.

6. Room Occupancy

Maximum occupancy is 2 adults and 2 children per room. Additional guests may require an additional room.

7. Smoking Policy

All rooms are non-smoking. Smoking is only permitted in designated outdoor areas. A $250 cleaning fee will be charged for smoking in rooms.

8. Pet Policy

Pets are not allowed in the hotel. Service animals are welcome with proper documentation.

9. Cancellation Policy

Cancellations must be made 24 hours prior to check-in to avoid a one-night charge. No-shows will be charged the full amount of the reservation.

10. Hotel Policies

Guests must comply with all hotel policies and local laws. The hotel reserves the right to refuse service to anyone.

11. Liability

The hotel is not responsible for lost, stolen, or damaged personal property. Guests are responsible for any damage to hotel property.

12. Privacy Policy

Your personal information will be used only for the purposes of your stay and will not be shared with third parties without your consent.

13. Digital Key

By using our digital key service, you agree to keep your mobile device secure and report any issues immediately.

14. Contact Information

For questions or concerns, please contact the front desk at (555) 123-4567 or email us at info@alphahotel.com.
